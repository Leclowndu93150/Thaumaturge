# Credits

Thaumaturge is a port of Thaumcraft by Azanor. Every texture, model and sound that was copied from Thaumcraft has been either redrawn for this project, confirmed as coming from a third-party source listed below, or removed.

Paths are relative to `src/main/resources/assets/thaumaturge/`. Everything not listed here is original work made for Thaumaturge.
